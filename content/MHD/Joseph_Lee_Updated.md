My therapeutic approach is integrative, meaning I tailor my clinical skills to meet your unique needs. I believe healing happens most effectively in a judgment-free space in which each person’s experience and perspective is honored. My goal is to help you feel deeply seen and known. <br>
I specialize in Christian counseling and welcome the opportunity to incorporate a faith-based perspective into our work together. Your faith can be an incredibly powerful resource in your healing, and I am here to help you draw strength from it in a way that feels authentic to you. <br>
I am also passionate about helping Asian Americans navigate the silent burdens of cultural expectations and find a safe space to process their pain. Many of us grew up in emotionally suppressed environments where keeping the peace, meeting high expectations, and burying our struggles were survival mechanisms. We were taught how to perform, how to endure, and how to serve—but perhaps not how to safely feel, process pain, or express our authentic selves. <br>
I offer a compassionate, culturally nuanced space to process intergenerational trauma and untangle spiritual exhaustion. Together, we can unpack the unique cultural and family dynamics that taught you to silence your own voice. If you are ready to move past performance-based living and cultivate a deeply rooted, emotionally healthy life, I would love to walk alongside you. Please reach out via email or visit my website to schedule a consultation call. <br>
