For people who want to understand themselves, not just manage what's happening on the surface. <br>
Some people come to therapy because something is clearly wrong. <br>
Others arrive with a harder-to-describe feeling. Life is moving forward, but they keep finding themselves in the same relationships, the same doubts, or the same emotional places without fully understanding why. <br>
Those are the conversations Nadia enjoys most. <br>
Nadia Bilodeau's Story <br>
Nadia is a therapist trained in Psychoanalysis who believes lasting change begins with curiosity. Rather than focusing only on what's happening today, she's interested in how earlier experiences, relationships, and ways of coping continue to shape the present. <br>
Much of her clinical work has been with adolescents and young adults, giving her a deep appreciation for the ways identity develops over time and how experiences earlier in life continue to influence adulthood. <br>
Whether someone is navigating a current challenge or trying to understand patterns that have followed them for years, Nadia approaches the work with patience, thoughtfulness, and genuine curiosity. <br>
What It's Like to Work With
Nadia Bilodeau <br>
Nadia creates space for people to think out loud, notice connections they may not have seen before, and explore questions that don't always have immediate answers. <br>
Rather than offering quick solutions, Nadia helps clients better understand themselves, their relationships, and the experiences that have shaped the way they move through the world. <br>
Many people appreciate having a place where they don't feel pressured to have everything figured out. Over time, that understanding often makes it easier to approach relationships, decisions, and challenges with more confidence and clarity. <br>
Nadia works with adolescents, young adults, and adults navigating anxiety, depression, OCD, postpartum experiences, relationship concerns, and family conflict. <br>
She also enjoys working with people who find themselves asking deeper questions about identity, relationships, and the patterns that seem to repeat throughout their lives. Some have tried therapy before and found it helpful, but still feel like there's more they want to understand about themselves.
