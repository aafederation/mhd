Shruthi Nair is the founder of Bold Brown Therapy a culturally informed practice providing virtual sessions in NY and PA, in person sessions in NYC. Shruthi's practice is grounded in supporting South Asian and BIPOC individuals struggling with anxiety, depression, life transitions, body image, people pleasing, Intergenerational trauma, and chronic health conditions. I provide a holistic, collaborative, and integrated approach. My therapeutic modalities that I use in sessions include CBT, DBT, IFS, and EMDR.
