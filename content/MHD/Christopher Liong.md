I’m a clinical psychologist who helps adults navigate anxiety, mood changes, stress, grief, and difficult life transitions. With a background in health psychology, I have a particular focus on the emotional and practical challenges of managing medical conditions and health changes. I have worked with people affected by chronic illness across many medical settings, and have specialized clinical and research experience in Parkinson’s disease. <br>
My approach is collaborative and grounded in cognitive behavioral therapy, and I incorporate mindfulness-based approaches when helpful. We’ll work together to understand what you’re experiencing, identify where you feel stuck, and develop practical strategies to move forward. Depending on your needs, we may focus on managing worry, building motivation, navigating life changes, or establishing manageable routines. <br>
I provide online therapy for adults in New York and New Jersey, and am in-network with Aetna. Reach out through my website for a free 15-minute consultation. <br>
