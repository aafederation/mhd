Alif Ahmed, LCSW, is a Bangladeshi American psychotherapist and the founder of Alifa Therapy, a multilingual practice based in Midtown Manhattan with telehealth across six states. He works with adults on anxiety, depression, trauma, relationships, and the strain of migration. Having gone through the asylum and immigration process himself, he pays close attention to how legal precarity, family expectations, and religious life shape what a person feels able to say out loud in a therapy room. His approach draws on cognitive behavioral, mindfulness based, strengths based, trauma informed, and spiritually informed methods, and he adjusts the frame to the client rather than the other way around. He began clinical work as a Behavioral Health Specialist with ThriveNYC, holds an MS in advanced clinical social work from Columbia University, and is completing a PhD at NYU Silver School of Social Work, where his research examines immigration policy and mental health access. He also teaches as adjunct faculty at NYU and Fordham. Sessions are available in English, Bangla, Hindi Urdu, and American Sign Language.
