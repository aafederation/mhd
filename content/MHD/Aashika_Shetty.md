Aashika is a Licensed Master Social Worker (LMSW) dedicated to working with individuals, couples, and families, with a particular passion for supporting underserved communities, immigrants, and couples and families of color. She brings compassion, cultural humility, and strong relationship-building skills to all her clients. She earned an MSW from the NYU Silver School of Social Work; her clinical training includes psychodynamic therapy, Emotionally Focused Therapy, Trauma-Focused CBT, and attachment-based approaches, giving her a flexible, evidence-based toolkit. Before entering clinical practice, she worked as a middle school teacher in Brooklyn. Her therapeutic experience includes supporting individuals, couples, and families navigating anxiety, relationship challenges, behavioral concerns, life transitions, and family stressors, with compassionate, collaborative care. As a first-generation South Asian American, she is passionate about serving immigrant and minority communities and integrating collectivist values into her therapeutic approach. She strives to create a space where clients can explore the intersection of culture, identity, family expectations, and relationships, and she enjoys helping couples and families navigate these dynamics while fostering deeper connection, understanding, and resilience. <br>
Specialties: Cognitive behavioral therapy, depression, anxiety, couples therapy, family therapy, immigration/cultural assimilation, family stress, BIPOC-affirming therapy <br>
