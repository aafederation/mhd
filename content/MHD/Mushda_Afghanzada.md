Life can feel overwhelming when you're navigating anxiety, depression, trauma, stress, relationship challenges, or major life transitions. You don't have to go through it alone. My goal is to provide a warm, supportive, and nonjudgmental space where you feel heard, understood, and empowered to make meaningful changes in your life. <br>
I believe therapy is a collaborative process tailored to your unique experiences, values, and goals. Together, we'll explore the patterns that may be keeping you stuck while developing practical coping skills to help you build resilience, confidence, and emotional well-being. I incorporate evidence-based approaches, including Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT), Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), mindfulness, and person-centered therapy. <br>
As an Afghan American therapist fluent in English and Dari, I understand the importance of culturally responsive care and the unique experiences that can come with balancing multiple cultures, family expectations, identity, and life transitions. I also understand conversational Hindi and Urdu, which can help foster comfort and connection for some clients, although I primarily provide therapy in English and Dari. I welcome adults from all backgrounds and am committed to creating a safe, respectful space where you can heal, grow, and feel supported. <br>
