You may find yourself moving between different worlds: family, culture, sexuality, spirituality, relationships, or expectations of who you’re supposed to be. Sometimes those parts fit together easily. At other times, they can leave you feeling pulled in different directions. I help clients navigate how our histories and relationships shape what we experience in the present, not only in our thoughts, but in our bodies and the ways we relate to ourselves and others. I work especially with immigrants, LGBTQIA+ people, and BIPOC individuals navigating identity, intimacy, family, belonging, and change. <br>
I offer therapy in English, German, Hindi, Punjabi, Spanish, Hindi, and Urdu. My approach is trauma-informed, relational, and psychodynamically oriented. Together, we might slow things down, notice recurring patterns, pay attention to feelings that are difficult to name, and become curious about what those experiences may be communicating. <br>
Ours will be a space where we can think and feel together, ask better questions, and make room for what may feel difficult, contradictory, or unfinished. Through that process, you can develop a deeper understanding of yourself and create more room for choice, connection, and a life that feels more fully your own. <br>
