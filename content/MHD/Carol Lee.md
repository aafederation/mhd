For more than a decade, I've sat with Asian and Asian American adults through high stress and anxiety, grief and trauma, the quiet exhaustion of always meeting everyone else's expectations, questions of identity and belonging, and the particular chaos of an ADHD brain. Many of my clients grew up in families where feelings often went unsupported and unprocessed. I understand that world, because I've moved through it myself. <br>
I meet you where you are, at the pace each session asks for. Like water, I find my way toward the quiet, overlooked places, drawn to what others might pass by, certain that each person already carries a current of their own. <br>
