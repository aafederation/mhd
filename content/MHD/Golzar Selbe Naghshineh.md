Golzar Selbe Naghshineh, M.S, LP, CGP, is a Training and Supervising Licensed Psychoanalyst and Certified Group Psychotherapist licensed in New York and New Jersey.\ <br>
Naghshineh has special expertise in reproductive and maternal mental health. She developed NAPS – an integrative mental health program for OBGYN offices and fertility clinics – that launched in 2014 at the renowned Downtown Women OBGYN practice in New York City. She partners with fertility clinics to provide group therapy treatment to their IVF patients. <br>
Naghshineh completed her psychoanalytic training at the Center for Modern Psychoanalytic Studies and her group psychotherapy training at the Center for Group Studies. She also attended the Boston Graduate School of Psychoanalysis for her doctorate in Psychoanalysis  where her dissertation examines the impact of online, virtual and hybrid psychoanalytic group therapy models. Naghshineh also holds a master’s degree in Nonprofit Management from the New School University. <br>
Naghshineh has presented at American Psychoanalytic Association (APsA), International Psychoanalytical Association (IPA), Austen Riggs Grand Rounds, Jones Foundation Infertility Counseling Conference (JFICC), Society for Psychotherapy Research (SPR), and the American Group Psychotherapy Association (AGPA), among other institutes and medical settings. Her most recent publication, in the journal Studies in Gender and Sexuality, is titled Psychoanalytic Considerations on the Impact of Infertility, IVF, and the Politicization of Pregnancy on the Psyche. She has several papers pending publication on group therapy, infertility, and working with IVF patients. She teaches psychoanalytic institute courses on both group therapy and identity formation in fashion. <br>
Her in-person private practice is in Union Square in New York City where she works with individuals, couples and groups. <br>
