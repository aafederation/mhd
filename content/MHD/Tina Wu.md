I'm a Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner and the founder of a private mental health practice in New York. I believe that mental health is just as important as physical health, yet it's often something we don't openly talk about, especially within the Asian community. <br>
I'm passionate about breaking that stigma and creating a welcoming, judgment-free space where people feel comfortable sharing their struggles and being themselves. My approach is warm, down-to-earth, and personalized because I believe everyone deserves to feel heard, understood, and supported. <br>
My hope is to help make conversations about mental health feel more normal, approachable, and less intimidating, one conversation at a time. <br>
