I'm Maria, I'm biracial and a psychotherapist based in Brooklyn, NY. I specialize in working with people who have experienced trauma or who are struggling with the impact of their symptoms on their relationships. Often times this can look like struggling with confrontation or speaking up for yourself, or having an unkind inner monologue; it can look like people-pleasing or avoiding a situation altogether. I also work with clients who are non-white and navigating identity and the effects of how they see themself vs how they are perceived by others.
\<br?>
I have been an EMDR provider since 2020. This intervention is especially interested in somatic information, in what you notice in your body when you encounter stressful situations in your life or think about past experiences. <br>
I am a New York and Massachusetts licensed psychotherapist who has worked in the mental health field for over 15 years. I offer free consultations, please reach out if you're curious about working together. <br>
