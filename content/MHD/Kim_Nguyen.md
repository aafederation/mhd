Living between languages, cultures, and identities can bring experiences that feel too difficult to put into words. You might find yourself caught between different roles and family expectations, only to realize that something still feels lost in translation, no matter what language you speak. Sometimes, these “in-between” feelings resurface during major life transitions, like changes in your career, relationships, or the path to parenthood. Such seasons can leave us anxious, depleted, disconnected, and longing to feel more at home in ourselves. I offer an affirming space for those who have experienced burnout, trauma, exclusion, or the complex journey of finding where they belong. Together, we’ll make sense of your story, untangle what feels complicated, and deepen your self-understanding and self-compassion. <br>
Informed by my Vietnamese heritage and my experience as an immigrant, I help clients weave together the many parts of who they are, honoring where they come from and who they are becoming. My approach is relational, culturally attuned, and trauma-informed, grounded in psychodynamic and narrative therapy, somatic and mindfulness practices, and Eastern philosophies. I also speak Vietnamese and welcome clients who want to use it in session. <br>
My hope is that in this space with me, you'll feel genuinely seen and understood, perhaps in ways you haven’t experienced before. You can expect warmth, honesty, and a consistent presence, without needing to explain or translate yourself. Whatever has brought you here, you're welcome. I'd love to hear from you. <br>
