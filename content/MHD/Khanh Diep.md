Everyone has their own inner healing wisdom, but sometimes, painful events in life cloud it. In therapy, the safe set and setting is meant for your wisdom to emerge and strengthen, so you can carry this wisdom along your journey of life. Perhaps, allow it to reunite you with your most important values, or to seek refuge and find calmness when challenges occur. I work with people who carry complex trauma, intergenerational burdens, high achievers burn out and with LGBTQ+, BIPOC identities. My approach is drawn from parts-work and somatic experiencing approaches. I am also a recent graduate from Columbia University’s psychedelic therapy training program.
