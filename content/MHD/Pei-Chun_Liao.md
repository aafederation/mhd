Throughout my career, I've trained in various settings, including university counseling center, college hospital, and schools, embracing a holistic approach to mental health. My goal is to create a safe, culturally sensitive space where we can collaboratively develop strategies that foster resilience, well-being, and personal growth. I've honed my skills in helping individuals understand their mental health, utilizing their strengths while developing new skills to enhance their quality of life. I am passionate about supporting marginalized students who face stigma in healthcare or academic settings. Using a culturally responsive model, I integrate cognitive-behavioral and interpersonal approaches into treatment, centering on your goals. Whether you're dealing with anxiety, depression, relationship issues, or other mental health concerns, I am here to support you every step of the way.
