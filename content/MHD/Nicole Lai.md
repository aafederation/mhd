Hi, I’m Nicole. I am a Licensed Master Social Worker (LMSW) dedicated to providing warm, relational, and culturally responsive therapy to children, adolescents, and families. <br>
Growing up in a first-generation immigrant family, I experienced firsthand the complexities of navigating bicultural identities, traditional family expectations, and intergenerational mental health challenges. This deeply shapes my practice. I specialize in helping young people and families unpack immigration trauma, manage school and life stress, and heal from family conflict. <br>
My approach is deeply collaborative and non-judgmental. I strive to create a safe space where your unique story is validated, meeting you where you are to encourage meaningful healing, growth, and empowerment. <br>
