I work with women who are often the ones others depend on — capable, responsible, and thoughtful — yet privately carrying anxiety, emotional exhaustion, relationship struggles, or the weight of family expectations. As a biracial woman and the child of immigrant parents, I understand the complexities of navigating cultural identity, belonging, and the expectations we carry across generations. <br>
Licensed in NY, NJ, and CT, I integrate attachment-focused therapy, IFS, and EMDR to help clients understand deeper emotional patterns and move toward greater clarity, self-understanding, and emotional steadiness. For those who desire it, faith can also be thoughtfully integrated into therapy. <br>
