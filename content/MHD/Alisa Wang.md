I often think about a quote by the English psychoanalyst Donald Winnicott: “To my client, who paid to teach me.” It reminds me that therapy is a two-way process of learning and growth. I work well with individuals who are trying to understand themselves more deeply and create a meaningful narrative about who they are. I see therapy as a mirror - one that helps you come closer to your true self with compassion and honesty. <br>
Having lived in different cultures and experienced what it meant to shift from being similar to many to being similar to few, I bring a sensitivity to questions of identity, relationships, and transition. My therapeutic approach is grounded in psychodynamic and analytic theories, and I like to integrate IFS language to explore different parts of your world. <br>
Together, we will create a space where all parts of you are welcomed, and you can truly be heard without judgment. In that space, we can explore how your past may still live within you today, and how your relationship with yourself shapes the way you connect with others - so you can find more clarity, ease, healing, and a deeper sense of self-understanding. <br>
