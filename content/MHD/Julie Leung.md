I am a Licensed Clinical Social Worker (LCSW-R) with over 30 years of experience providing psychotherapy to adults. As a Chinese American immigrant, I bring a personal understanding of the complexities of cultural identity, acculturation, intergenerational expectations, and the challenges of navigating multiple cultural worlds. <br>
My practice specializes in complex and relational trauma, attachment difficulties, anxiety, depression, and dissociation. I integrate trauma-informed, somatic approaches, including Brainspotting, to help individuals process unresolved experiences, develop greater emotional regulation, and foster a deeper connection with themselves. <br>
I am especially passionate about supporting Asian American, immigrant, and BIPOC communities, providing a culturally sensitive, compassionate, and collaborative therapeutic environment. I am licensed in New York and New Jersey and offer psychotherapy exclusively through telehealth. <br>
