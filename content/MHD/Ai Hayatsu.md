How does your sticky thought move? What color is your sadness? Does your joy illuminate? Imagery and creativity give us a new language to communicate with our inner selves. In the therapeutic space, I witness your pain with compassion, encourage different perspectives, and sometimes challenge your narratives. I have experience working with diverse populations, helping them navigate various challenges such as depression, anxiety, grief, and identity exploration. Together, we create a space to explore your unique healing paths. <br>
As a first-generation immigrant myself, I understand what it's like to hold multiple conflicting values and feel trapped between them. <br>
I hold people’s resilience and ability to cope with grief close. Grief exists in many places in our lives, like shadows of every life transition we experience. Grief marks changes and achievements in our lives, and when we separate them from shame or regret, they can become messages for our future selves. <br>
I work with individuals, couples, and groups, bringing in my expertise in listening and guiding you to develop your own tools to paint your inner world. I integrate art therapy, somatic work, and mindfulness practice in my work. Finding the right therapist is a vital step to healing. Please reach out for a free 15-minute consultation to learn more about my work. <br>
