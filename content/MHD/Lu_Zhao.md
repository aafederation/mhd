Lu has worked with individuals, couples, and families for over 12 years, in clinics, in people's homes, and in community agency settings. She works with people from all backgrounds and has particular depth with immigrant families, Asian and bicultural families, Latinx families, and couples navigating real differences in culture and expectations. <br>
Her work is rooted in a family and relational approach. Even in individual sessions, she explores the relationships and dynamics that shape the person in the room. She has deep experience helping families navigate the larger systems in their lives, like schools and other services, and find solid footing within them. She is also particularly attuned to immigration-related stress and trauma, and brings a trauma-informed lens to her work. <br>
Lu is an immigrant herself, bilingual in Mandarin and English, and has spent most of her career in Brooklyn. She is trained in Structural Family Therapy and is an AAMFT Clinical Fellow and Approved Supervisor. <br>
Many of her clients return years later when life brings new challenges. That kind of trust is something she takes seriously. <br>
