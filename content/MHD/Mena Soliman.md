My work is warm, collaborative, and centered on creating a space where you feel comfortable being yourself. I believe therapy should feel supportive and practical, while still giving you the tools and encouragement to make meaningful changes. Together, we’ll focus on what you’re experiencing, what matters most to you, and what feels manageable at each step. <br>
I draw from approaches such as Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, Exposure and Response Prevention, Prolonged Exposure, Acceptance and Commitment Therapy, and Motivational Interviewing, tailoring my work to your individual needs rather than taking a one-size-fits-all approach. My goal is to help you feel understood, supported, and empowered as you move toward the life you want. <br>
