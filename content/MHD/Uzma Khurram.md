Are you someone who looks like they have it all figured out on the outside — successful career, the right relationship, status — and yet you feel anxious or unfulfilled? Have you been navigating a major life transition recently? Are you caught between two cultures or trying to find peace and purpose within a chaotic and unpredictable world? Wherever you are in your journey, please know that I see you and I understand. <br>
I offer a holistic, personalized approach to therapy. By combining psychotherapy with other modalities, I seek to get to the root of the issue. I help my clients work through their traumas and chronic patterns, examine self-limiting beliefs, release emotions stuck in the body, and become aware of conditioned, repetitive behaviors that no longer serve them. <br>
I am here to offer you a nurturing and non-judgmental space in which you can begin to heal. I am a licensed psychotherapist in New York and California. Currently, I offer virtual sessions for clients who reside in these two states. Schedule a free 15 minutes initial consultation to find out how we can work together. <br>
