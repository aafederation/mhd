Rini Adhikari is an ICF Professional Certified Coach (PCC), Executive and Life Coach who believes that meaningful change begins with greater self-awareness, courage, and the freedom to redefine success on one's own terms. <br>
With over two decades of corporate leadership experience across Asia and the US, Rini brings a unique blend of business insight, cultural sensitivity, and human understanding to her coaching. Having navigated cultures, careers, and continents herself, she understands the complexities of identity, ambition, belonging, and life transitions. <br>
Her coaching goes beyond professional success. She partners with individuals navigating career crossroads, personal reinvention, relationships, confidence, and the often-unspoken pressures of balancing expectations with personal aspirations. <br>
Warm, intuitive, and gently challenging, Rini helps clients question limiting beliefs, discover new perspectives, and make choices that feel authentic to who they are. <br>
Based in New York City, Rini is the founder of The First Circle, holds an MBA from IIM Ahmedabad, and serves on the Board of ICF New York City. <br>
