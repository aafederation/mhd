If you’re carrying the weight of anxiety, adjusting to a new country or a new chapter of life, or balancing family expectations with your own needs, I’m here to help. Many people in immigrant and multicultural communities are taught to keep struggles private. I offer a space where you can speak openly, without shame or judgment, and where your culture, family, values, and faith are respected as part of who you are. <br>
I work with adults facing anxiety, depression, low self-esteem, life transitions, and the stress that can come with immigration, acculturation, discrimination, and generational differences within families. Using a blend of Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), mindfulness, psychoeducation, and narrative therapy, we’ll work together at your own pace to build coping tools, self-awareness, and lasting resilience. <br>
I’m a Licensed Master Social Worker (LMSW) in New York, working toward my LCSW, with a background in Forensic Psychology and a Master’s in Social Work from Adelphi University. I provide therapy in English and Spanish, and I’m committed to culturally humble, community-centered care. I believe healing isn’t linear, and seeking support is a sign of strength, not weakness. <br>
