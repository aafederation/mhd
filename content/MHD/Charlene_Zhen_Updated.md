I'm a psychodynamic therapist in New York City working with adults and couples. On the surface you appear capable and successful. Underneath, you feel anxious, burnt out, or disconnected, moving from one achievement to the next without pausing to feel satisfied. For a long time you could manage it. Lately it comes out in ways even you don't recognize. <br>
Many of my clients are Asian American and adult children of immigrants, carrying family pressure, cultural expectations, and the weight of being the one who was never supposed to be a problem. Many are also realizing later in life that they are neurodivergent, whether ADHD, autistic, or both, and that a lifetime of masking was how they coped. I also see couples, including interracial and cross-cultural couples. <br>
My approach is psychodynamic, culturally informed, and reflective. We look at how family dynamics, cultural context, and early experiences shaped the person you became. As those patterns come into view, you move from reacting to choosing, and get clearer about what you want and freer to say it to the people in your life. My own perspective is shaped by being the eldest daughter in a Chinese immigrant family, and by eight years in tech startups before this.
