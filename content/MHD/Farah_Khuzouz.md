My ideal clients are individuals and couples feeling emotionally disconnected, stuck in painful patterns, or overwhelmed by relationship, family, or cultural pressures. Many are high-functioning externally but struggling internally with communication, intimacy, anxiety, or identity. I help clients better understand themselves, their relationships, and the patterns shaping their emotional lives while building healthier ways of connecting and communicating. <br>
I specialize in working with individuals and couples navigating relationship struggles, emotional disconnection, attachment wounds, cultural identity, and life transitions. My approach is relational, reflective, and culturally responsive, helping clients deepen insight, improve communication, and create more fulfilling relationships. <br>
You may feel emotionally exhausted, disconnected in your relationships, or stuck in patterns that repeat despite your efforts to change them. Therapy can offer a space to feel understood, gain clarity, and reconnect with yourself and others. If you’re seeking a warm, culturally responsive space to process, I’d be happy to connect with you. <br>
