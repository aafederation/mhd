I’m Amber-Rose Marmol, LMHC, CHt, a Korean and Dominican therapist and the founder of Love Bug Therapy. I provide warm, culturally responsive telehealth therapy for adults and couples located in New York, specializing in anxiety, trauma, relationship concerns, identity, and major life transitions. <br>
I understand that healing does not happen separately from culture, family expectations, intergenerational experiences, or the identities we carry. My integrative, trauma-informed approach may include EMDR, clinical hypnotherapy, somatic work, mindfulness, DBT, and narrative exploration. I strive to create a space where clients do not have to minimize, translate, or disconnect from parts of themselves to feel understood. <br>
My practice is affirming of LGBTQIA+ and neurodivergent clients, chosen families, and diverse relationship structures. <br>
