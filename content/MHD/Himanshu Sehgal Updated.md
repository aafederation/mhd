Heal with Himanshu: Where Compassion Meets Clinical Excellence in Mental Health. <br>
Himanshu is a dynamic therapist with years of expertise in the field of social work and psychotherapy, offering compassionate, individualized care at The Room for Therapy. With a master's from Stony Brook University's School of Social Work, he specializes in anxiety, depression, ADHD, grief, trauma, and many co-occurring conditions, connecting deeply with patients in English, Hindi, Urdu, Bengali, and Punjabi. <br>
Himanshu has been recognized for his innovative therapeutic approaches, receiving accolades from professional associations for his contributions to mental health. His commitment to continuous learning has led him to present at international conferences, further establishing his reputation as a leader in the field. <br>
Using evidence-based methods like Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Psychodynamic Therapy, Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), Trauma-Focused therapy, and Motivational Interviewing, he helps patients and couples uncover strengths and build sustainable coping skills. His trauma recovery approach creates a safe space to process experiences, integrating mindfulness and grounding exercises to empower resilience. <br>
What sets Himanshu apart is his unwavering cultural competency and adaptability, ensuring every patient feels seen and respected. Choosing Himanshu is a transformative step toward a healthier, more authentic life—guided by warmth, expertise, and genuine support. <br>
