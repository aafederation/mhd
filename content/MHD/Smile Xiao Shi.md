I'm a NYS licensed mental health counselor. By day, I have the privilege to direct a graduate training program for future therapists at a community mental health clinic serving AAPI and immigrant communities. By evening, I work at a group practice providing evening therapy to busy New Yorkers. I'm passionate about working with AAPI students, international students, children of immigrants, queer folks, and others who often feel caught between different cultures and places. Emotional regulation and academic organization challenges are also areas of my interest and expertise. I'm extensively trained in Accelerated Experiential Dynamic Psychotherapy (AEDP).
