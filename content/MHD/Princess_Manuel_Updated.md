Are you managing a demanding life but internally struggling to juggle it all, feeling disconnected from your body, and wondering why you keep repeating the same chaotic relationship patterns? If you find yourself constantly people-pleasing, struggling to set boundaries without intense shame, or feeling torn between a mind that says "stay" and a body that says "leave," you are likely experiencing the constriction of survival mode. When you have survived childhood or recent chaos, your nervous system learns to equate safety with staying small, quiet, or hyper-functional, which can sever your connection to intimacy, pleasure, and your natural vitality. My practice is designed to help you decode your body's somatic intelligence, heal womb and intimacy trauma, and dismantle the self-abandonment that keeps you feeling numb, so you can transition from merely surviving to feeling genuinely alive. <br>
True healing requires looking beyond the individual to the historical, systemic, and generational traumas—including the ongoing impacts of systems of colonization—that pressure us to perform, conform, and over-produce. As a client in this space, you are stepping into the role of a generational cycle breaker, deconstructing these internalized systems and honoring the resilience of ancestors who loved the best way they knew how, even without the clinical language to name their pain. In our work together, we will alchemize this pain in a compassionate, non-judgmental container, building the discernment skills and somatic integration necessary to reclaim your boundaries, restore your instinctual "wildness," and confidently step into your power. <br>
I am Princess Manuel, a Licensed Clinical Social Worker (LCSW) specializing in relational, trauma-informed therapy grounded in a deep understanding of interlocking systemic oppression and the history of marginalized communities. The therapeutic relationship we build is deeply relational, intuitive, and human—an alchemical space that artificial intelligence can never replicate, serving as the very laboratory where your healing takes place. If you are ready to stop pleasing everyone else, reclaim your spark, and co-create a liberated future, contact me today to schedule your initial consultation. <br>
