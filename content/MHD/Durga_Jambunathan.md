Hi! I'm Durga. I'm a licensed mental health therapist specializing in ADHD, anxiety, and identity exploration. Honestly, I am open and excited to work with anyone who is willing to go deep and be present in the therapy "room". I hold an Ed.M. in Mental Health Counseling from Teachers College, Columbia University, and a B.S. in Psychology and Gender Studies from Kansas State University. I have a background in social psychology research which often helps contextualize us in the socio-political space. <br>
I am an eclectic therapist, drawing mainly from Narrative, Relational, and Parts Work theories. My approach to therapy is making space for us to play, get curious, and to honor and give space to all parts of you. I believe in working with people beyond western ideas of what therapy “should” look like, working from a social justice and multicultural framework.
