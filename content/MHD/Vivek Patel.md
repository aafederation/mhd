Hi, my name is Vivek, and I’m a dual-certified Psychiatric-Mental Health and Family Nurse Practitioner with a passion for providing thoughtful, culturally responsive mental health care. My approach is holistic and collaborative, taking the time to understand each person’s story, culture, experiences, values, and goals. I have a particular interest in supporting Asian American and South Asian communities, as well as individuals navigating anxiety, depression, life transitions, relationships, identity, and antidepressant deprescribing. I incorporate mindfulness, self-reflection, and, when meaningful to the individual, spirituality or faith into care. Above all, I hope to create a warm, open, and unrushed space where people feel heard, understood, and supported in making meaningful change.
