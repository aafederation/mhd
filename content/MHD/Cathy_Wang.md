I am a bilingual therapist offering sessions in English and Mandarin, and a New York State Licensed Mental Health Counselor (LMHC). My therapeutic style is warm, curious, and collaborative, grounded in the belief that we have a profound capacity for change through connection, understanding, and care. Drawing from psychodynamic, relational, and humanistic approaches, I work with individuals navigating relationship challenges, codependency, complex trauma, anxiety, depression, identity, family conflict, and life transitions. As a queer immigrant, I bring both clinical experience and lived understanding of the complexities of identity, belonging, and connection. My hope is to offer a space where all parts of you are welcome, and where you can discover new possibilities for living and relating more fully as yourself.
