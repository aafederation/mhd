I work with adults navigating trauma, anxiety, relationship difficulties, and questions around identity and self-understanding. I have a particular interest in how past experiences shape the way we see ourselves, relate to others, and move through the world. I also value supporting clients from Asian and Asian American communities, first-generation individuals, and those navigating intergenerational family dynamics, cultural expectations, and the tension between honoring family and finding their own path. My background in forensic psychology and mental health counseling informs my understanding of the ways our experiences shape our emotional lives and relationships. I am trained in EMDR and strive to create a space where clients feel comfortable exploring difficult experiences without judgment. <br>
My approach is warm, collaborative, and trauma-informed. I draw from EMDR, CBT, ACT, attachment-based approaches, and mindfulness to help clients understand their experiences and find new ways of responding to challenges. I am interested not only in the symptoms someone is experiencing, but also in understanding the patterns beneath them and how those patterns may have developed. Together, we can explore how past experiences, relationships, and expectations influence the present while building practical tools for managing emotions, setting boundaries, and feeling more grounded. I believe therapy should move at a pace that feels manageable, balancing deeper exploration with meaningful, lasting change. <br>
Your first session is an opportunity for us to get to know one another and begin understanding what brings you to therapy. You do not need to have everything figured out or know exactly where to start. We will talk about what has been feeling difficult, what you hope might change, and any experiences or patterns you would like to better understand. I welcome questions and want you to feel comfortable sharing your perspective, including what has or has not been helpful in the past. My goal is to create a space where you feel heard and respected, without pressure to share more than you are ready to. We will begin identifying what feels most important to you and consider how we can work together toward your goals. <br>
