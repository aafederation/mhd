Hello, I'm glad you're here! I'm Rachel, a culturally-responsive and LGBTQIA+-affirming clinician, and I received my Master of Social Work from Columbia University. Therapy with me is warm, authentic, and laid-back. It's a space where you can slow down from life's chaos, start getting unstuck, and deepen your relationship with yourself and others. I specialize in working with Asian women, children of immigrants, individuals navigating multiple cultures, young adults, and creatives. My focus is on supporting clients with anxiety, life transitions, intergenerational trauma, family and relationship challenges, and identity exploration. Overall, I enjoy working with people who are eager to explore mental wellness through a system lens, learn more about themselves, and live with more clarity and intention. I truly believe that therapy is more than just learning how to cope – it's about experiencing lasting transformation and joy, and I'm here by your side through both the messiness and the growth.
