Musarat Yusufali is a Licensed Clinical Social Worker. She began her career in Computer Science at UT Austin and after a few years of working in tech, she realized her passion and mission was elsewhere. She then pursued her Masters in Social Work at Columbia University and that began her journey and passion in helping building peaceful and loving families. She does this by focusing her therapeutic work on healing trauma and families have better relationships with each other. She loves the science of the brain and incorporates many of the sciences into her work. She’s married in a multi-cultural dynamic and raising two beautiful children in Northern Virginia.
