I’m a board-certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner providing culturally responsive, trauma-informed care to adults. As an Asian provider, I understand how culture, family expectations, immigration experiences, and identity can shape the way we experience and talk about mental health. My approach combines thoughtful medication management with supportive therapy, with particular interests in anxiety, depression, ADHD, trauma, and complex psychiatric medication management. I strive to create a warm, nonjudgmental space where patients feel understood as a whole person—not simply as a diagnosis.
