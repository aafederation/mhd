I am a second-gen, queer, and neurodivergent Korean-American raised and educated in NYC. My specialization in clinical neuropsychology and trauma-informed psychotherapy is ideal for individuals living chronic illness, neurodiversity, and/or other marginalized identities. My practice focuses on the intersectionality of these identity-related factors and how they inform interactions with the self, others, and broader environment. What I can uniquely offer is holistic and expert care in Asian American mental health, LGBTQIA+ challenges, neurodiversity, perfectionism and burnout in "high performers," veteran mental health, and sexual and intimacy issues. <br>
I have a decade of experience with integrative, strengths-focused, and trauma-informed healing modalities (ACT, CBT, DBT, IFS, and Eastern contemplative practices), as well as culturally sensitive neuropsychological evaluations. I have a special interest and training in conducting Adult ADHD and Autism evaluation and Executive Functioning Coaching. <br>
